About us

A Midrand glass company you can reach directly.

NN Glass (Pty) Ltd is based in Midrand, Gauteng. We specialise in glass repairs and replacements, mirrors and custom glass, window and door installations, and glass and stainless-steel balustrades.

You deal with us from the first message to the final fitting, and we would rather show you our finished work than describe it. That is why the gallery on this page only shows our own projects.
